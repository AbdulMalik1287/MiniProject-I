Neurological disorders

Separate GNNs for Alzheimer's, frontotemporal dementia and Parkinson's, each trained on one hospital's patients against that hospital's own controls. Different hospitals, older patients and, for Parkinson's, eyes-open recordings: these results are not comparable with the psychiatric models above, and no model is asked to choose between diseases from different hospitals.
